THE POWER OF A FIRST HELLO

Student + mentor · Sample conversation

Demo

Every beginning starts with hello.

  1. Student

    Hi! I have a research idea, but I'm not sure where to start.

  2. Mentor

    That's okay! Every great project starts somewhere. Tell me about your idea.

  3. Student

    I want to create a flood-monitoring system that could help our community.

  4. Mentor

    That's a meaningful idea! Let's explore what technology and research methods could make it possible.

  5. Student

    Really? I thought it might be too difficult.

  6. Mentor

    With the right guidance, we can break it down into achievable steps. 🌱
